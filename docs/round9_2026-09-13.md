# Round 9 (2026-09-13): the regenerated corpus, a known-gravity input, and the feet

Start: the round-8 recipe (`configs/r8/F2m_scale03.yaml`) and its force anatomy
(`docs/round8_force_anatomy.md`: hands right, feet 0.75 of the GT at every quantile, climb_wall_2
right foot 0.33 of its board). The user regenerated the corpus (1419 train / 109 test scenes, was
864 / 107) with a better hands / feet distribution in the kindyn forces. Configs `configs/r9/`,
runs `output_4/`, logs `output_4/logs/`.

## What was built

* `scripts/data/precompute_pose_tokens.py` (ported into main, live backbone): frames extracted and
  pose tokens computed for the 557 new scenes (~25 frames/s/GPU; reproduces the old cache to 1e-4).
  `scripts/predict_test.py` dumps now read the cache too (3.5 GB, seconds per scene; differences to
  the live pass: joints 0.3–1.6 mm, contacts 0.001, forces 0.001 bw). Stage 1 stays the old one.
* **Optional known-gravity input** `model.refiner.gravity_input {enabled, p_given, measured_only,
  eval_given}`: four token channels (the given gravity in the body frame + a flag). In training each
  clip whose corpus gravity is a measurement is given it with probability `p_given`; a given clip's
  gravity output IS the input (the head is bypassed, the per-layer feedback uses it, the gravity loss
  skips it). At inference `eval_given` hands every measured clip its gravity; the BVR trees' ground-plane
  gravity counts as measured. `configs/r9/{B,C,D}_given_eval.yaml` are the given-gravity twins,
  `*_predict.yaml` the cache-free inference twins.
* **Joint-torque distribution term** `force_consistency.loss.joint_torque` +
  `force_consistency.joint_torque_multipliers`: `RootWrench.residual` now also returns the 21 joints'
  torques from the same RNEA; the term is the BVR `torque_magnitude` (multiplier × squared torque,
  bw·m at a 1 m lever; hip / knee / spine 1, ankle / foot 1.7, shoulder / collar 2.5, elbow 3.3, wrist
  17) on the DETACHED body with live, contact-gated forces. Metrics `joint_torque` / `gt_joint_torque`
  (the kindyn GT costs 0.062 (bw·m)² on the GT body, so the term has a floor).

## Reading

* More data alone (A) is worth ~0.4° and 0.002 bw; the pose is 0.2 mm better and contact F1 0.007
  lower on the re-labelled split — none of it moves the feet.
* The joint-torque term (C) re-allocates by LOWERING THE HANDS: the corpus hands ratio goes 1.01 → 0.94,
  the feet stay at 0.8, and on the boards the net force drops (0.91 → 0.87 bw). Nothing held the total:
  the root-residual force part sat at 0.30 bw with δ 1.0 and the supervised loss is per limb.
* Pinning the total (D: supervised net force + a tight root residual) and supervising the toes'
  MAGNITUDE turns the same term into a re-allocation: corpus feet 0.79 → 0.87 at hands 0.95, feet share
  = GT; boards hands/feet 1.55 → 1.23, right foot 0.39 → 0.50, left foot 0.58 → 0.71, net force back to
  0.95 bw, MAE 93 → 83 N, share error 6.7 → 3.9 pp, correlation 0.53 → 0.62 — the closest learned row to
  the optimisation so far. The price: force direction +1.1° on the corpus (the direction of the
  supervised loss now weighs magnitude), off-contact force 0.029 → 0.037 bw (hands the boards say are
  free read 100–125 N), and the contact transitions lose what C had gained (transition F1 −0.035).
* The toe group weights on the vector Huber alone (B) did nothing (feet 0.78 → 0.78).
* The known-gravity input costs nothing when off (B = A within noise) and is worth 0.004–0.006 bw and
  0.8–0.9° of force when given; the head itself still returns the camera axis ± a few degrees.
