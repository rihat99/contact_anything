## 1. Where the jitter is — oracle decomposition

The lifted world joints are `J_w = R_cw^T (R_cb L + p − t_cw)` with `p` the camera-frame pelvis
(CLIFF lift), `R_cb` the root rotation and `L` the root-local joints. Each predicted component is
replaced by its GT in turn and GVHMR jitter recomputed (static baseline; anchor_raw in brackets):

| variant | jitter | torso | extremities | WA-MPJPE100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| prediction | 107.9 (83.6) | 99.1 | 125.3 | 95.5 (89.6) |
| GT depth along the predicted ray (2D unchanged) | 49.2 (43.7) | 29.2 | 85.7 | 56.2 |
| GT pelvis (full) | 44.4 (38.6) | 21.3 | 84.6 | 54.2 |
| GT root rotation | 105.5 | 95.3 | 125.8 | 94.7 |
| GT local pose | 104.3 | 99.7 | 112.8 | 82.2 |
| everything GT except **depth** | 90.9 (66.7) | 90.2 | 92.4 | 72.4 |
| everything GT except local pose | 37.9 (34.2) | 13.5 | 81.0 | 52.2 |
| everything GT except root rotation | 33.8 (28.7) | 19.3 | 57.2 | 25.1 |
| everything GT except lateral pelvis | 21.6 (20.7) | 20.1 | 24.4 | 17.8 |
| all GT | 6.7 | 4.8 | 10.2 | 0 |
| prediction, depth smoothed σ 0.12 s | 49.7 | 30.1 | 85.7 | 91.5 |
| prediction, pelvis + rotation smoothed σ 0.12 s | 39.4 | 16.2 | 81.3 | 91.8 |
| prediction, everything smoothed σ 0.12 s | **9.2** | 9.0 | 9.4 | 91.7 |

The single-component rows add in quadrature to the total (`sqrt(91² + 38² + 34² + 22²) = 107`):
the four noise sources are independent, and the **camera-frame pelvis depth alone is worth 91 of
the 108**. Local-pose noise lives in the extremities (81 vs 13 torso). Post-hoc σ 0.12 s smoothing
of all components reaches the GT jitter without moving WA-MPJPE — the information for a smooth
trajectory is there; nothing in the network uses it.

## 2. What the depth noise is (and is not)

`z = 2 f / (b s)`: `b` the square crop side (per-frame SAM3 bbox), `s` the head's crop scale.

| check | baseline T=60 | T=1 | train clips | anchor_raw | frozen SAM3D |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| jitter | 107.9 | 113.0 | 97.2 | 83.6 | (MHR70 body) 116.8 |
| jitter, GT body + this model's depth | 90.9 | 94.8 | 85.9 | 66.7 | 85.5 |
| depth error, fast (< 0.25 s) RMS | 0.044 m | 0.045 | 0.044 | 0.038 | 0.037 |
| depth error, slow RMS | 0.211 m | 0.221 | 0.105 | 0.204 | 0.191 |
| frame-to-frame Δz RMS (GT 0.015) | 0.027 | 0.028 | 0.035 | 0.021 | 0.024 |
| slope Δlog s / Δlog b | −0.96 (r −0.98) | −0.97 | −0.95 | −1.01 | — |

* **Not the bbox.** The per-frame bbox side jitters by 3.3 % per frame, but the head compensates
  it with slope −0.96 (r −0.98): `b·s` is insensitive to the crop. Bbox smoothing would not help.
* **Not our head.** The frozen SAM 3D Body camera head, read from the same token, has the same
  fast depth noise (3.7 cm) and the same depth-only jitter (85). This is the token's per-frame
  depth ambiguity floor; the anchor run already sits on it (3.8 cm).
* **Not overfitting.** Train-clip fast noise equals test (4.4 cm). Only the SLOW depth error is
  memorised (10.5 cm train vs 21 cm test) — the frozen model has 19 cm on both.
* **The temporal block smooths nothing.** Cutting the clips to T = 1 changes the jitter by 5 %
  (113 vs 108) and the fast depth noise not at all. It is a per-frame fix, as found before.
* **The CLIFF math is right.** `cliff_cam_to_translation` / `translation_to_cliff_cam` are the
  textbook conversion and exact inverses; the 2D term projects with the true intrinsics and then
  the crop affine, consistent with the wrapper's crop. The pelvis-relative `kp3d` term is blind to
  the pelvis (it subtracts it), it does not carry its error; absolute depth is held only by `cam`
  (Huber on `s`, gradient 0.6) and, since the anchor arm, by the pelvis anchor.

## 3. Why the losses do not ask for smoothing

The per-frame losses reward denoising only in proportion to the noise's share of the error
variance. Depth: fast 0.044 m vs slow 0.21 m → the fast part is ~4 % of the depth-error variance,
and the depth terms are themselves a small part of the total. The jitter metric, by contrast, is
100 % the fast part. So the optimizer has essentially no incentive to build a temporal denoiser
out of the 1024-d residual RoPE block — and that block would have to learn a near-identity
1024×1024 value map (`Δ_t ≈ mean_local(x) − x_t`) from zero-init gates to do it, which it never
did (it converged to a per-clip constant offset). Pushing harder through the same path (matching
×3) degraded the per-frame pose instead.

Motion matching + the pelvis anchor (anchor_raw) reduced the fast depth noise 0.044 → 0.038 and
the jitter 108 → 84: the loss side works, the architecture has no cheap lever.

## 4. Fix under test: a learned convex output smoother

`model/smoother.py` — `y_t = Σ_k w_tk x_k`, `w_t = softmax_k(−Δt²/2σ_g² + β_g [k = t])` over the
clip's frames, time-valued (fps-agnostic), one `(σ_g, β_g)` per output group (cam, root, body,
betas, hands). Convex → it can only average per-frame predictions, never offset them; identity at
init (β = 4, σ = 0.1 s → 95 % self weight); clip mean in the limit. Applied to the SMPL-X head's
raw outputs (`model.smplx.smoother`), own optimizer group at `optim.smoother_lr_scale` (Adam moves
these scalars by ~lr/step; at 2e-4 they could not leave the init within a run).

Arms (static subset, 30 epochs, GPUs 0+5 / 4+6):

* SM-0 `configs/static_smoother.yaml` — baseline + smoother (control: do the per-frame losses alone
  widen the kernel?).
* SM-1 `configs/static_smoother_matching.yaml` — anchor_raw (matching 1 / 0.5 / 1 + pelvis 3) +
  smoother.

Success bar: lifted jitter far below 84 with MPJPE / WA no worse than the run without the smoother;
the learned σ per group is reported.

## 13. Why the transformer does not smooth — the bbox probe, and what GVHMR actually does (2026-09-04, day)

**Question (user):** the block has the capacity to average across frames; why does it need an output
smoother? **Hypothesis 1 (missing input):** the block runs in token space, where depth lives as the
CROP-relative scale `s`; the lift `z = 2f/(b s)` applies the bbox side `b` only afterwards, in the SMPL-X
head. Averaging `s` across frames re-injects the ~3 %/frame bbox jitter (post hoc: depth jitter 91 → 112,
vs 10.6 averaging `z`), and the block never sees `b`. **Hypothesis 2 (structure):** the block is
`x + γ·attn(x)` on the RAW frozen token; a convex average would have to cancel the token's own noisy
depth exactly, whereas context pooling (what the diagnostic measured: same-frame mass 1-3 %, half the mass
beyond 20 frames) is what an additive branch finds easy.

**Probe:** `configs/static_anchor_raw_bbox.yaml` — `model.token_inputs.bbox`: the CLIFF vector
`[(cx-px)/f, (cy-py)/f, log(b/f)]` through a zero-init MLP ADDED to the pose token before the block
(GVHMR's `cliffcam_embedder`, which uses `b/f`). Control = `static_matching_anchor_raw` (same 8 clips/step).

| run | jitter | depth-only | lateral | rot | local | MPJPE / PA | depth err / bias | WA / W | RTE | F1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| control (anchor_raw) | 84.1 | 66.7 | 20.7 | 28.7 | 34.2 | 65.0 / 47.0 | 145 / +25 | 89.6 / 131 | 4.75 | 0.829 |
| + bbox input | 77.9 | 59.7 | 20.5 | 28.4 | 34.6 | 66.2 / 47.2 | 161 / +54 | 91.4 / 138 | 4.84 | 0.834 |
| SM-6 (output smoother) | 9.7 | 9.9 | 10.2 | 7.6 | 6.3 | 61.7 / 44.2 | 132 / +21 | 83.5 / 137 | 4.42 | 0.855 |

(decomposition columns from `jitter_decomp.py`, `onlyXPred` variants; the control row is the `anchor`

**What GVHMR does (hmr4d source, `scratchpad/gvhmr/`):**
- Its per-frame token is NOT the image feature. `NetworkEncoderRoPE`: the MAIN token is the 2D
  keypoints (COCO-17, `2(uv-c)/b`, joints with conf ≤ 0.5 or outside the bbox replaced by learned
  per-joint params, `Linear(2,32)` → MLP `544 → 1024 → 512`); ADDED through zero-init final layers:
