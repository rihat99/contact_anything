# Round 8 (2026-09-12): gravity, contacts and forces on the smooth body

Start point: the round-7 body `configs/smooth/P_k30.yaml` (`output_3/P_k30_20260912_011321/best.pth`,
54.86 mm / jitter 5.87 / pelvis 110, contact and force heads off). Plan and decisions:
`docs/round8_plan.md`. Configs `configs/r8/`, runs `output_3/<arm>_<stamp>/`, evaluations
`output_3/evals/<arm>_{best,last}.json`, paired bootstraps `output_3/evals/paired_<arm>.json`
(`scripts/paired_ci.py`, reference P_k30, capped protocol). GPUs 3 / 4 / 6 / 7, one arm each, 30 epochs,
cached pose tokens, 8 clips per GPU, `best.pth` by lifted jitter for the pose-only arms and by contact F1
from C1 on.

## 1. Questions

1. Can the model predict the gravity direction instead of reading the corpus value?
2. Do the contact and force heads work on the learned-smoothing body, and what do they cost it?
3. Does contact consistency (in-contact stillness, forward stencil) help?
4. Do the RNEA residual as a per-layer FEEDBACK and / or as a LOSS improve the forces?
5. Does frame masking close the train / test gap?

## 2. What was built (all uncommitted on main at the time of writing; 68 tests)

* `model/physics.py` — `RootWrench`: the RNEA root-wrench residual (moved out of
  `force_consistency`, which delegates), shared by the loss and the refiner feedback.
* `model/refiner.py` — `camera_axes` (the camera's down and viewing axes in the body frame, 6
  channels); a `gravity` output (per-layer zero-init head predicting a body-frame correction on the
  camera's down axis; unit votes pooled per clip in the world, normalised, falling back to the pooled
  camera axis when the votes cancel; `out["gravity"] = {world, body, prior_world, world_layers}`);
  every non-pose head read after every layer under `iterative` (`logits_layers`, `forces_layers`),
  the contact probabilities and body-frame gravity fed back to the next layer; `residual_feedback`
  (RNEA residual of the layer's body under its contact-gated forces and its predicted gravity —
  body, gate and gravity detached, forces live — 6 channels into the next layer; zero for clips
  shorter than the ±2 stencil); `frame_mask_p` (training-time replacement of a frame's input token by
  a learned embedding); `head_grad_scale` (factor on the gradient the non-pose heads, their fed-back
  values and the gravity pooling's rotation send into the trunk: 1 shared, 0 probes).
* `model/loss/gravity.py` — `1 − cos` per clip on scenes whose gravity was measured, deep-supervised;
  metrics `angle_measured` / `angle_fallback` and the camera-axis prior's `prior_angle_*`.
* Loader flag `gravity_measured` (GeoCalib `source != fallback_down`); `contact_consistency.stencil:
  forward`; `layer_weight` deep supervision for the contact and force losses; config validation.
* Codex (gpt-6-astra) review of the refiner changes: 9 findings, 7 fixed (unit-vote pooling with a
  fallback, short-clip residual, iterative needs ≥ 2 layers, layer-weight validation for decoder heads
  and force terms, gravity as a monitor, detachment and pooling tests), 2 kept on purpose: the RNEA
  acceleration stays the ±2-frame stencil (Nyquist-blind — the stage-1 wobble must not become fake
  inertia), and the gravity metrics are means (the additive-stats interface carries no medians).

## 3. Two facts about the data

**The corpus gravity is a placeholder on 40 % of the scenes.** `features/geocalib/<scene>/gravity.npz`
(copied into `kindyn_1.npz`) has three sources: a fitted ground plane (49 test / 365 train scenes),
pooled per-frame GeoCalib (16 / 171) and `fallback_down` = world +y (43 / 328). The world frame IS
the first camera (`extrinsics[0]` = identity), so the fallback is the first camera's down axis, not a
measurement. On the ground-plane scenes the two independent estimates (ground normal vs pooled
GeoCalib) disagree by 22.7° median / 46° p90 on test (10.4° / 54° train): the "measured" target is
itself uncertain by 10–20°.

**The camera's axis is the gravity cue.** Against the measured targets, the camera's down axis is
12.2° off (median; mean 13.65°, p90 30.5°), the GT body's own up axis 36°, a ridge regression from
GT pose features 12.2° (mean; median 8.3°). Cameras filming climbers point up by ~12°; climbers lean
and hang.

**The test set changed mid-round.** 56 test scenes' `annotation.npz` were re-edited between 13:25
and 14:40 and `scenes/scenes.db` at 14:40 (108 → 107 test scenes). Each run reads the test set at
launch: G0–F0 and C2h use the old labels and 108 clips, F2 / F3 partially edited labels, F2h / R1 /
F2m and every post-run evaluation after 14:40 the 107-scene split (P_k30 re-scored there:
53.88 mm / jitter 5.61 / pelvis 109.4). The user judged the label edits minimal and asked for no
re-evaluation; the tables below mark the split.

## 5. Reading

**Gravity.** The corpus gravity channel was never needed (G0 = P_k30 to the noise). The learned
head ends at 12.8° on measured scenes against the camera axis's 13.65° (means): a 1° gain, inside
the 10–20° uncertainty of the targets themselves. On fallback scenes it moves 10° away from the
first camera's axis, which is what a learned "cameras point up" correction does and cannot be scored
there. The head costs the pose 1 jitter unit on the shared trunk because the gravity loss reaches the
body rotation through the lift (pooling in the world with the layer's rotation); with the coupling
scaled (C2h, F2h) that path is cut. Deployment no longer needs the kindyn gravity.

**Contact.** On the learned-smoothing body the contact head reaches F1 0.93 in five epochs, above the
round-6 bar of 0.927 on the Gaussian body, with precision 0.93. Its gradient shapes the shared trunk
and costs 3.5 jitter units at unchanged MPJPE. A fully detached probe keeps the pose but plateaus at
F1 0.877 (C1d), so the trunk does need some contact gradient; a 0.1 gradient scale (C2h) keeps F1
0.927 and gives the lowest jitter of the round (5.4 at best.pth, 5.1 at the minimum).

**Stillness.** The forward-stencil in-contact speed loss brings the predicted extremity speed to and
below the GT floor (0.17–0.19 vs 0.194) and counteracts most of the contact head's jitter (C1 9.4 →
C2 6.6), at a cost of 0.25 mm MPJPE [0.12, 0.44] — the one real accuracy cost in the round. Its
target is stricter than the labels' own definition (the GT moves at 0.19 m/s "in contact").

**Forces.** On the shared trunk the force head matches the round-5 MAE (0.184) and is 1.8° worse in
angle. The RNEA residual as a LOSS is the one force change that helps: MAE 0.179, angle 20.3°
(−1.5°), for +0.007 bw of off-contact force and a residual of 0.27 bw against the 0.21 GT floor. The
RNEA residual as per-layer FEEDBACK (F1) is a small consistent MAE gain on its own (−0.003, six
times the seed spread) with the angle unchanged, at +10 % epoch time, and adds nothing on top of the
loss (F3 = F2). The head coupling is a straight trade: 0.1 buys the smoothest body (jitter 5.1–5.5
at best.pth, 4.8 at the minimum) for +0.005 MAE; 0.3 keeps F2's forces (0.179 / 20.6°) with F2h's
jitter (5.5) and P_k30's MPJPE on the same split (53.98 vs 53.88). Two seeds of F2m agree to 0.005 mm,
0.07 jitter, 0.001 F1, 0.001 bw and 0.2° at best.pth.

**Frame masking.** Replacing 10 % of the input tokens by a learned embedding during training
changes nothing: R1 = F2h on every metric, and the train / test loss gap is the same. The
regularisation already in place (dropout, weight decay, EMA, window jitter) is what there is.

**The recipe.** `configs/r8/F2m_scale03.yaml`: P_k30 + camera axes + gravity head (weight 5, measured
scenes) + contact head + forward-stencil stillness + force head + RNEA loss, deep supervision 0.5 on
every head, `head_grad_scale 0.3`, `best.pth` by contact F1 (epoch 16–17 of 30). Run
`output_3/F2m_scale03_20260912_154016`; against P_k30 on the same split it gives up 0.1 mm and
0.1 jitter for contacts at F1 0.927, forces at 0.179 bw / 20.6° and a predicted gravity, with no
corpus gravity at inference.

## 6. What is left open

* The gravity targets: 40 % placeholders and 10–20° disagreement between the two measurements. A
  better target (a per-scene gravity from the wall geometry, or the GeoCalib estimates re-pooled with
  the camera motion) is the prerequisite for any further gravity work.
* Contact precision on the heels stays 0 (heels 3.5 % positive); unchanged from round 6.
* The stillness cost (0.25 mm) vs its benefit is a judgement call: it is a prior the labels themselves
  do not satisfy.
